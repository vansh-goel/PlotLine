# Today’s plot

- Mode: Operate
- Audience: connected and prospective builders
- Job: understand today’s theme, inspect the shared build, select a piece, place it validly, and see contribution/payout state
- Primary action: place the selected piece
- Constraints: mobile-safe, keyboard-visible, contract rules mirrored, exact 90/10 split, no invented activity

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The shared 3D plot is a working site model, not content inside a dashboard. Refuse the generic game HUD and the equal-card SaaS shell.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm drafting-paper surfaces, near-black graphite type, field-green baseplate, and one construction-orange action color. Fine rules, coordinate ticks, survey labels, and squared controls make a precise working instrument without imitating a real construction set.

**STORY:** The visitor immediately sees what everyone is building today, learns whether a placement is valid, connects a wallet, and contributes. Allowance, builder count, countdown, and the transparent 90/10 payout stay readable without competing with the plot.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim top rail names PlotLine and holds wallet state. The 3D plot occupies roughly two-thirds of desktop width and most mobile height. A compact right workbench holds theme, time, allowance, shape, rotation, color, and the place action. The snapped ghost is the signature survey-marker interaction.

**FORM:** Survey Studio, grounded candidate 7, seed `f4491c29`. Plot coordinates drive labels and controls; restrained density from the Japanese high-density challenger raises information efficiency, while the star-atlas challenger contributes ranked dot/marker hierarchy without its dark palette.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
